Pemayangtse Monastery

Location: Pemayangtse, Gyalshing, Sikkim, India | Nearest Towns: Gyalshing (6 km), Pelling (44 km)

❮ ❯

🕉️ History

Pemayangtse Monastery, one of the oldest and most revered in Sikkim, was founded by Lama Lhatsun Chempo in 1647. Initially established as a small Lhakhang, it was expanded in 1705 during the reign of the third Chogyal, Chakdor Namgyal, and consecrated in 1710 by Lhatsun Chenpo Dzogchen Jigme Pawo. It follows the Nyingma Order of Tibetan Buddhism and historically governed other monasteries of that order in Sikkim. The head lama had the privilege of anointing Chogyals with holy water. "Pemayangtse" translates to "Perfect Sublime Lotus."

🏰 Architecture

  • Three-storied structure at 2,085 meters elevation with snow-capped mountain backdrop.
  • Paintings on walls depicting Tibetan Buddhist themes.
  • Statues of saints and Rinpoches across floors.
  • Main prayer hall (Dukhang) with colorful doors and windows in Tibetan design.
  • Statue of Padmasambhava in wrathful form as Dorje Bhurpa Vjarakila with two consorts.
  • Eight fierce incarnations of Padmasambhava displayed in murals.
  • Manicured gardens and residential quarters for monks.

🎉 Festival

  • Cham Dance Festival held annually on 28th and 29th day of the 12th Tibetan lunar month (usually February).
  • Monks dress as Mahākāla and Guru Drag-dmar (Vajrakila) in colorful costumes.
  • Pilgrims from all over Sikkim gather to witness ritual dances.
  • Large embroidered scroll displayed on the last day.
  • Fireworks mark the conclusion, driving away evil spirits.

🕒 Visitor Information

  • Opening Hours: Daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM
  • Entry Fee: INR 20 per person
  • Activities: Observe daily rituals, explore Dukhang, admire murals, statues, gardens, and panoramic views of Mount Khangchendzonga.
