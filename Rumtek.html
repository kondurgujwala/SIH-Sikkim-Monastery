Rumtek Monastery

Location: Rumtek, East Sikkim, India | Nearest Town: Gangtok (24 km)

❮ ❯

🕉️ History

Rumtek Monastery, also known as the Dharma Chakra Centre, was originally founded in the 16th century by the 9th Karmapa Wangchuk Dorje. In the 1960s, it was rebuilt under the guidance of the 16th Karmapa, Rangjung Rigpe Dorje, to serve as the seat of the Karmapa Lama in exile. The monastery is a significant center for the Karma Kagyu lineage of Tibetan Buddhism and houses rare Buddhist religious artifacts and texts.

🏯 Architecture

  • Main Shrine Hall (Dharma Chakra Centre): Large prayer hall with intricate murals and statues of Buddhist deities.
  • Golden Stupa: Enshrines relics of the 16th Karmapa, serving as a meditation focal point.
  • Karma Shri Nalanda Institute: Buddhist study center, open to visitors 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM.
  • Kora Path: Circumambulatory path for meditative walking with panoramic mountain views.

🎉 Festivals

  • Chaam Dance: Traditional masked dance during Tibetan New Year (Losar) and Saga Dawa.
  • Karmapa’s Birthday: Celebrated with prayers and rituals honoring the Karma Kagyu spiritual leader.

🕰️ Visitor Information

  • Opening Hours: Daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM
  • Entry Fee: ₹10–20 per person
  • Activities: Participate in daily rituals, explore prayer hall, admire murals, statues, gardens, and enjoy mountain views.

🛤️ How to Reach

Rumtek Monastery is located about 24 km from Gangtok, the capital of Sikkim. Visitors can reach it by taxi or public bus from Gangtok.
