Enchey Monastery

Location: Gangtok, East Sikkim, India | Nearest Town: Gangtok (3 km)

❮ ❯

🕉️ History

Enchey Monastery, meaning "Solitary Monastery," was established in 1840 by Lama Druptab Karpo, a revered tantric master known for his flying abilities. The current structure, built in 1909 in Chinese Pagoda style, stands at the location of Lama Druptab Karpo's hermitage. It belongs to the Nyingma order of Vajrayana Buddhism and houses around 90 monks. It is believed to be the residence of protective deities Khangchendzonga and Yabdean.

🏯 Architecture

  • Two-story structure with a golden cupola at the top.
  • Walls adorned with murals depicting Mahayana Buddhist deities.
  • Four elaborately carved pillars supporting the roof.
  • Serene surroundings with colorful prayer flags fluttering around.

🎉 Festivals

  • Detor Cham: Cham dance festival on 18th–19th day of 12th lunar month (January/February) with mask dances portraying mythological stories.
  • Singhe Chaam: Dance performed by monks symbolizing victory of good over evil.
  • Pang Lhabsol: Commemorates blood oath between Bhutia and Lepcha communities with Khangchendzonga as witness.

🕒 Visitor Information

  • Opening Hours: Daily from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
  • Entry Fee: Free
  • Activities: Observe rituals, explore Dukhang, admire murals, statues, gardens, and enjoy panoramic views of Mount Khangchendzonga.
