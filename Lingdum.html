Lingdum Monastery (Ranka Monastery)

Location: Ranka, East Sikkim, India | Nearest Town: Gangtok (approx. 20 km)

❮ ❯

📜 History

Lingdum Monastery was established in 1999 under the guidance of H.E. Zurmang Gharwang Rinpoche, the 12th throne holder of the Zurmang Kagyu lineage. It serves as a center for monastic education and spiritual practice, following the Zurmang Kagyu tradition of Tibetan Buddhism. Located in Ranka village, approximately 20 km from Gangtok, it offers a peaceful retreat for locals and visitors.

🏛️ Architecture

  • Main Prayer Hall (Dukhang) with vibrant murals and statues.
  • Statue of Guru Padmasambhava, a prominent figure in Tibetan Buddhism.
  • Prayer Wheels and Chortens scattered throughout the monastery grounds.
  • Residential quarters housing monks and students.

🎉 Festivals

  • Gutor Festival: Celebrates the Tibetan New Year with traditional rituals, prayers, and cultural performances.
  • Other Buddhist festivals with masked dances and community rituals.

🕒 Visitor Information

  • Opening Hours: Daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM
  • Entry Fee: Free
  • Activities: Participate in or observe daily prayers and rituals, explore architecture and surrounding landscapes, interact with resident monks.
