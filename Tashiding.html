Tashiding Monastery

Location: Tashiding, Gyalshing, Sikkim, India | Nearest Towns: Gyalshing (27 km), Yuksom (16 km)

❮ ❯

🕉️ History

Tashiding Monastery, founded in 1641 by Ngadak Sempa Chempo Phunshok Rigzing—one of the three wise men who consecrated Sikkim’s first king—is a revered center of Tibetan Buddhism. Expanded and renovated in 1717 during the reign of the third Chogyal, Chakdor Namgyal, it is situated on a hilltop at the confluence of the Rathong and Rangeet rivers, offering panoramic views of valleys and Mount Khangchendzonga.

🏯 Architecture

  • Three-storied structure with intricately painted Tibetan designs in the main prayer hall (Dukhang).
  • Statues of Guru Padmasambhava and other revered figures.
  • Thong-Wa-Rang-Dol Chorten, a sacred stupa believed to cleanse sins with a glance.
  • Mani stone slabs inscribed with Buddhist mantras along pathways.
  • Surrounding stupas and prayer flags enhance the spiritual ambiance.

🎉 Festival

  • Bumchu Festival celebrated annually on 14th–15th day of the first Tibetan month (usually February).
  • Sacred vase filled with holy water is opened to predict the coming year's prosperity.
  • Devotees from across the region gather for the ritual ceremony.

🕰️ Visitor Information

  • Opening Hours: Daily from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM
  • Entry Fee: Free
  • Activities: Observe rituals, explore Dukhang, admire murals, statues, gardens, and enjoy panoramic views of Mount Khangchendzonga.

📍 How to Reach

Tashiding Monastery is approximately 27 km from Gyalshing and 16 km from Yuksom. Visitors can reach it by taxi or private vehicle, enjoying scenic views along the journey.
